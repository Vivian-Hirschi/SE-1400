Our Mission

Schedule Planner was built to help students and busy individuals stay organized with simple, clean tools. Our mission is to make planning easier, reduce stress, and help you stay focused on what matters most.

What We Offer

Our planner includes a calendar for long-term planning, a task list for daily responsibilities, and a schedule page for hour-by-hour organization. These tools work together to help you stay productive and in control of your time.

Why We Built This

This project was created as part of a web development course, focusing on building real, functional pages using HTML and CSS. Every part of this site was designed to be clean, accessible, and user-friendly.
